Five heroes for the status page

Each one sits on the real page grounds and uses the live Moooom tokens, so what you judge here is what ships. Pick a number.

01

Machine room

hero-1.svg

The closest answer to GitHub's scene: engineers tending a contraption that feeds a film reel through to a screen. Busiest of the five, and the only one with a story in it.

02

Isometric rack

hero-2.svg

Reads as infrastructure rather than media: a rack with a drive pulled halfway out, patch cables arcing to a display. The teal drive lights are the only saturated marks.

03

Signal flow

hero-3.svg

No characters. A signal leaves the tower noisy and resolves into a clean line as it passes the relays. The quietest option, and the one that will age best.

04

Retro cinema

hero-4.svg

A projector throwing a beam across the full width onto a screen. The beam is the only large filled shape on the page, so the accent carries it and everything else stays outline.

05

Mascot relay

hero-5.svg

Round characters passing media down a line into the screen. Thickest strokes of the five, and the only one that is actually trying to be funny. Someone has dropped a disc.

Tell me a number and I will copy it to tools/assets/hero.svg, rebuild, and push it to the open pull request.

None of them is committed as the hero yet, so choosing costs nothing and changing your mind later costs one line. If none of them land, say what is wrong and I will redraw rather than pick the least bad one.
